## 📈 4-Point Likert Scale

For each test question, a value will be scored according to the response, mapped below:

(3 points) Concordo plenamente!
(2 points) Concordo, até certo ponto.
(1 points) Mais discordo do que concordo.
(0 points) Discordo totalmente!

## 📊 Score Calculation

To calculate the score for each dimension, sum the respective points according to the response (see scale) for each of the 8 questions in each dimension. Then, totalize and divide by 24; multiply by 100 and round to obtain the percentage to be presented in the result for each dimension.

    dimension-index = round-integer ( Sum of question-points / 24 * 100 )
