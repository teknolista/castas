## 🛢️ Data Structure

At the end of the test, the result will be stored in the `castas_struct` structure and saved in the browser's localStorage. The pcIdxDims field has 5 integer values, corresponding to the score of each dimension. The timestamp field records the date and time when the test was performed.

    castas_struct
    	timestamp datetime
    	pcIdxDims int[5]
